Colony

How it works
  • Foragers (21+ bee-days old) fly out, land on open flowers and suck nectar. Crop load tops out at ~40 mg, and loaded bees fly slower.
  • Flower constancy: a bee keeps to one flower species per trip and remembers good patches.
  • Waggle dance on the comb: the run angle is the flower's direction relative to the sun, and the duration grows with load. Idle foragers that watch it fly to the spot.
  • Receivers take nectar from foragers and pack it into the top cells. Water falls from ~70% to ~18%, then the cell is capped.
  • Builders (12–20 bee-days) make wax from honey, about 8 kg honey per kg wax, so building stalls when stores run low.
  • Task choice follows response thresholds: each bee reacts to the colony's current need.

Speeds (m/s), loads and sugar levels are realistic. Bees, comb and flowers are drawn enlarged, and time runs fast (one day ≈ 4 min).
